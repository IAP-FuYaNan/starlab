About Me

I am a postdoc researcher working with Prof. Jianhua SUN at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My current research focuses on understanding the physical mechanisms involved in mesoscale processes, such as convection initiation and upscale organization over extratropical continents. If you are interested in my research, please do not hesitate to contact me and we can have a discussion.

Research Interest

  • Pre-convective Environments

    Pre-convective environments refer to the atmospheric conditions that precede the development of thunderstorms and other forms of convection.

  • Convection Initiation

    Convection initiation (CI) refers to the process where a convective storm (a storm that develops due to rising air masses) first appears and develops.

  • Convection Organization

    ‌Convection organization‌ refers to the structured movement or circulation patterns that occur due to convection processes.

  • Evolution of Cloud Structures

    The evolution of cloud structures in MCSs involves complex microphysical and dynamical interactions, with key features observed across observational and modeling studies.
